9. PER-FILE INVENTORY

--- Browser.swift (2235): central state and every WebKit delegate ---

newTab (1002-1044)
- On a private tab it makes a private tab.
- Never two blank tabs: an existing blank tab is moved to the end and focused instead.

close (1084-1139)
- A floating tab lands first.
- Pinned tab: rest() it, then select the most recently touched awake loose tab.
- Last tab: if blank, close the window; otherwise replace it with a blank tab.
- Otherwise: add a ghost, then select the tab to the right.
- closeOthers rests pinned tabs instead of removing them.

Other tab operations
- reopen (1174-1190): inserts at min(index, count).
- move (1199-1210): pinned and loose blocks never mix.
- open(url, foreground, atEnd): inserts after the active tab.
- arrive(url) (an external link): reuses the active blank tab if nothing has been typed.
- visit(url): Ctrl held, or the current tab floating, opens a new tab.
- Pin (505-559): pin = monogram, moved to the end of the pinned block. letter() keeps the first non-space character, uppercased. unpin moves the tab to the head of the loose block.
- Tab edit (571-630): the draft is pretty(url). Rename with an empty name clears it. Click-away commits unless the draft is empty, unchanged or invalid.
- copyMarkdownLink: "[title](url)" with \ [ ] escaped (647-658).

Find (143-171)
- Case-insensitive, wraps around.
- Closing runs JS `window.getSelection().removeAllRanges()` (line 156).
- Port: WebKitFindController with CASE_INSENSITIVE | WRAP_AROUND, max 1000; failed-to-find-text marks it missed.

pauseMedia: JS `document.querySelectorAll('video,audio').forEach(m=>m.pause())` (used when switching spaces).

Passwords (278-416)
- The save offer needs: saving on, a non-empty password, not private, host not on the never list. An identical login is only touched.
- The account list:
  - Up to 5 logins via Vault.logins(matching).
  - Only when filling is on and the tab is active.
  - Hidden 0.2 s after the caret leaves the field.
- Toasts: "Password saved/updated for <host>", "The keychain refused it".

Clearing (427-451): webkit_website_data_manager_clear with ALL, or DISK_CACHE | MEMORY_CACHE | OFFLINE_APPLICATION_CACHE, on each space's session.

Capture (2018-2051)
- "permission-request" with a WebKitUserMediaPermissionRequest.
- The answer is remembered under capture.<host>|<type>.
- One question at a time; a concurrent request is denied.

decide-policy for navigation (1843-1936), in order:
1. Should download → download.
2. Middle button (2) → open a background tab and ignore.
3. Shift-click alone on http with peek on → peek.
4. Ctrl-click → new tab (foreground if Shift is held).
5. Main frame → re-arm the curtain CSS and tune Shield for the host.
6. Schemes http, https, file, about, data, blob are allowed; anything else → gtk::UriLauncher, then ignore.

"create" (1941-1964): a new tab with related-view = opener and its own user-content-manager. It inherits private mode and is activated.

decide-policy for responses (1967-1991)
- 3xx → allow.
- Content-Disposition "attachment…" → download.
- MIME type not supported → download.

load-failed (2122-2153)
- Ignore CANCELLED and error 102 (frame load interrupted).
- Otherwise tab.failure is one of:
  - "No site at that address."
  - "No connection."
  - "The site took too long to answer."
  - "The site refused the connection."
  - "The connection isn't secure." (also load-failed-with-tls-errors)
  - "The page didn't load."

Load events and closing
- "close" (2072): select the opener, then close the tab.
- COMMITTED (2083): dismiss the link bubble, clear the failure, apply the remembered zoom, remove the cover after 0.45 s.
- FINISHED (2105): remove the cover, settle the sign-in, fetch the favicon, record history unless private.

Downloads (2162-2230)
- network-session "download-started" → decide-destination.
- Asking where to save: GtkFileDialog. Otherwise pick a free name in the folder: "stem 2.ext", "stem 3.ext", …
- Toasts: "Downloading <name>", "Saved <name>", "Download failed".
- loot.add on finish.

--- Stage.swift (351): the page host ---

Layers
- The web view, absent while blank, asleep or floating.
- Cover image: fill, top-leading, no hit testing, easeOut 0.2 s.
- Floating placeholder: "This page is playing in the floating window." 13 pt muted.
- Error: message 14 pt ink, "Try again" 12 pt muted, 10 apart. Animated with quick.

Swipe disc (83-120)
- 52×52 circle in ground, 1 px hairline, shadow 12% blur 16 y 6.
- Progress arc: trim 0→grown, ink, 1.5 px, round caps, starting at 12 o'clock.
- Arrow 15 pt semibold, opacity 0.4 + 0.6·grown.
- Formulas:
  - reach = 150·(1 − e^(−travel/110))
  - grown = min(1, travel/110)
  - scale = going ? 1.08 : 0.86 + 0.14·grown
  - x = ±(10 + 0.2·reach + (going ? 12 : 0))
- Animation: easeOut 0.22 s on going; appear/disappear easeOut 0.16 s with scale 0.85.
- Phase 1: use enable-back-forward-navigation-gestures instead.

StageView: one slot, reconciled with set_page(Option<WebView>). Unparent the old view before parenting the new one; the float window does the same.

Drop: DragStrip (use GtkWindowHandle), WindowSetup, RestingLights.

First frame
- macOS holds the view at alpha 0 until first paint, then fades it in over 0.12 s.
- Port: webkit_web_view_set_background_color(ground), plus an optional 120 ms opacity fade on COMMITTED.

Custom swipe (1339-1585)
- arm 70 pt; flick 30 pt within 0.25 s; the disc shows after 6 pt.
- The axis is decided after 6 pt: across if |dx| > 1.3·|dy|. back = the first dx is positive.
- If the page doesn't answer within 0.18 s, it is assumed free.
- On a completed swipe the disc clears after 0.32 s.
- Touchpad scroll only, no momentum.
- Port: GtkEventControllerScroll in the capture phase plus "scroll-end". Late phase.

--- TabBar.swift (965): top strip (52) and shared tab widgets ---

Row, left to right, spacing 2
[100 pad] [SpaceDot + 2, if spaces are on] [tab run] [2] [plus 30] [spacer] [Helm, 8 trailing] [2] [Bookmarks door 26] [12 pad]
- Empty areas drag the window.
- Dropping a URL or text on the strip opens it; the strip turns hover while targeted.

Geometry (231-282), copy exactly
- room(W) = max(0, W − 100 − dot − 12 − 30 − far − 6)
  - dot = SpaceDot 26 + 2 when spaces are on, else 0
  - far = measured width, or 116 before measurement
- loose width = loose == 0 ? 186 : clamp((room − pinned·30 − (count−1)·2) / loose, 36, 186)
- content = pinned·30 + loose·width + (count−1)·2 + (editing ? min(340, W−112) − (edited tab's width) : 0)
- run = min(content, room)
- Overflowing when content > room + 0.5: scrolls with no bar; the active tab is scrolled into view (glide).
- While making a space, the run is min(540, room).
- Port: a custom container widget with measure/size_allocate inside a ScrolledWindow (hscrollbar EXTERNAL).

Animations on the strip: activeID glide (the pill slides), editingTab glide (widths), tab order settle.

Space paging
- The row is offset in y by spaceSwipe; the neighbours sit ±52 away.
- Past the last space: the inline NewSpaceCard.
- Clipped vertically.

Plus button
- Icon 10 medium muted in 15×15, padding 7/6, radius 9, hover fill.
- Visible only while the pointer is over the strip: opacity 0↔1 and scale 0.7↔1 from the leading edge, settle.

Helm
- Back / Forward / Reload, or Stop while loading. Tooltips "Back ⌘[", "Forward ⌘]", "Reload ⌘R", "Stop ⌘.".
- Disabled at opacity 0.3; a blank tab disables all three. Animated with quick.
- Door (Side.swift:759-785): 26×26, icon 11 medium, radius 8.
  - Colour: on ink, hover ink 0.7, else muted.
  - Background: on wash, hover hover.

TabPill (339-568)
- States:
  - pinned: 16×16 glyph, padding 7/6, width 30. Icon, or the letter 12 medium; opacity 0.45 asleep.
  - compact (< 80): Ring or Mark 15.
  - titled, HStack spacing 6: [Mark 15 if glyph = icons] [eye.slash 9 pt if private] [title 12.5, tail truncation] [spacer ≥ 2] [speaker] [15×15 slot].
    - The slot holds the cross when hovering (xmark 8 semibold muted, circle ink 0.07, hit area 30×28), or the Ring while loading.
    - Padding leading 11, trailing 7 (11 while editing), vertical 6.
    - Pill height 28.
- Background:
  - live: wash, radius 9, the sliding pill, plus a reading fill (ink 0.055, width × reading, easeOut 0.15; not on pinned or compact tabs, only if tabs.reading).
  - hover: hover fill.
  - resting pinned tab: wash at 0.55.
- Text colour: live ink, hover ink 0.7, else muted.
- One gesture per state:
  - live pinned tab: double-click edits the letter
  - live loose tab: click edits the address in place
  - not live: click selects
  - middle-release inside the tab closes it
  - right-click: TabMenu
- Shake on refusal. Insert/remove: scale 0.9 from the leading edge plus opacity.
- Port: draw the live pill as one indicator behind the children, animating x and width with a glide spring.

Carried, drag to reorder (583-633)
- Starts after 5 px. target = clamp(from + round(travel/step)).
- move is animated with settle. The held tab has z 1 and shadow 14% blur 12 y 4.
- step = (30 or width) + 2.

TabAddressField (635-743)
- 12.5 pt, no border, selection ink 0.11. Select-all on appear.
- Return commits; Esc cancels; focus loss or an outside click finishes.
- The SiteCard is anchored to it.

TabMenu (746-792)
- Pin, or Change Letter + Unpin
- ---
- Rename, Duplicate, Site Information…, Copy Address, Copy as Markdown Link, Mute/Unmute Tab
- ---
- Close Tab, Close Other Tabs, Reopen Closed Tab

Ring: arc trim 0-0.78, muted 0.7, 1.4 wide, round caps, size 10, 360° linear every 0.85 s. Custom drawing, not GtkSpinner.

PinField (881-965): 12 medium, centred, keeps one character. Return, Esc, Tab or blur ends the edit.

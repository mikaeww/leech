# Hand-off 2026-10-01: the owner's colours

## State
Settings › Colours is in (ADR 0008, plan `plans/2026-10-01-paint.md`, verification `verification/paint.md`):
look, eight presets, window as grey / colour / gradient (linear with angle, radial, dithered), accent, new tab
sheet, dot colours and dot size, Reset. `ui/paint/` holds the arithmetic (`colour.js`, unit-tested), the
application (`apply.js`), the picker (`picker.js`) and the presets. ADRs now live in `decisions/interface/` and
`decisions/chromium/` (the folder was at its limit).

## Decisions
- One paint for both looks; the ink (light or dark look's text) follows the colours, not the look.
- Leech's own picker: the UI's contents in the Chromium build have no colour chooser delegate.
- `--window` is image + size + colour; the Chromium build's chrome round the page is a masked ring placed by
  `--left` / `--stage-top` (render.js), because `fixed` does nothing on a masked layer.

## Open
- During a glide the gradient on a moving part moves with it for a moment.
- Dithered windows are busier behind text than smooth ones; contrast is judged on the stops only.
- The settings rail and the welcome keep their own colours; painting them is a choice not yet made.

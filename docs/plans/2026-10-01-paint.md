# Plan: the owner's own colours (2026-10-01)

Decision: [ADR 0008](../decisions/interface/0008-own-colours.md). Verification: [verification/paint.md](../verification/paint.md).

## Result
Settings gains a Colours page. On it: the look (light, dark, system), presets, and every colour by hand with a
picker drawn by Leech (a saturation and brightness field, a hue slider, a hex field):

- **Window**: the sidebar, the tab strip and the bookmarks bar, as grey, one colour, or a gradient of two or
  three colours, linear at any angle or radial, optionally dithered in Bayer dots.
- **Accent**: what is inverted today (the step on a rail, the one filled button, a switch that is on, the
  chosen suggestion).
- **New tab**: the sheet's colour, the picture's dots in one colour or two that shade across it, the dot size.

Text on a painted surface takes whichever ink (the light or the dark look's) reads better on it. Nothing set
means the window looks exactly as before. Changes show at once and are saved in `settings.json` under `paint`.

## Implementation
1. `ui/paint/colour.js`: hex, HSV, mixing, contrast, the ink for a set of colours; a leaf with unit tests.
2. `ui/paint/apply.js`: one `paint` object to custom properties and ink classes; the dithered gradient.
3. `ui/paint/picker.js`: the picker as a popover under its swatch.
4. `ui/paint/presets.js`, `ui/panels/settings/colours.js`: the page.
5. Styles: `--window` paints body, sidebar, strip, bar; in the Chromium build a masked ring round the stage
   replaces the spread shadow, since a shadow can't be a gradient.
6. `check:ui` scenario `paint`; `test/paint.test.mjs`.

## What can break
- The Chromium build's chrome around the page (the ring): checked in `check:ui --chromium`.
- The stock look: compared pixel for pixel before and after with nothing set.
